AllowMe turns good habits — reading, chores, learning — into real savings your
kids can see and grow. **You set the rules. Your kids earn. You approve.**

<Note>
  **You're always in control.** Parents decide what kids can do. Kids earn and
  settle within those rails. Money can only ever go to a destination you've
  authorized — nothing can be sent somewhere you didn't approve, by anyone.
</Note>

## See it in action

Before you set anything up, here's the whole AllowMe loop in one family's week.
Watch the money go from **earned** to **in the wallet** — only after a parent
approves.

That's AllowMe: **kids earn, parents verify, money settles on approval**, and
the difference between *earned* and *in your wallet* is always visible. No
surprises, no hidden balances.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose your role">
    AllowMe works a little differently depending on who you are. Pick the one
    that fits, then follow the steps inside.

    <Tabs>
      <Tab title="I'm a parent setting up for my family">
        You're the **Manager** — you create the family, invite everyone, and
        set the rules. Only this path starts with **Sign in with Base** on the
        verify page.

        <Steps>
          <Step title="Sign in with Base">
            1. Open **[allowme.dev/verify](https://allowme.dev/verify)**.
            2. Click **Sign in with Base** (not the invite-code box — that's for
               people joining an existing family).
            3. Approve the sign-in in your wallet. No payment, no gas — just a
               signature.
            4. Create your family on the next screen if this is your first time.
               When you're done, you'll see **your magic link** (like
               `https://allowme.dev/mcp?setup=SETUP-XXXX-XXXX`) and an **Open in
               Claude** button. Keep that link private.

            <Note>
              **Why do I sign in with a wallet first?** Your Base wallet becomes
              your family's secure owner. It's how AllowMe knows you're the parent
              in charge, and it's what keeps your kids' money safe — funds can only
              ever move to places you approve. You do this once, at the start.
            </Note>
          </Step>

          <Step title="Connect AllowMe">
            Use **your magic link** from the verify success screen — not bare
            `allowme.dev/mcp`.

            <Tabs>
              <Tab title="Claude">
                **Fastest path:** [Open AllowMe in Claude](https://claude.ai/customize/connectors?modal=add-custom-connector&connectorName=AllowMe&connectorUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fallowme.dev%2Fmcp%3Fsetup%3DSETUP-XXXX-XXXX) — same as **Open in Claude** on the verify page; the modal opens with your link pre-filled.

                **No button? Add manually:**
                1. **Customize → Connectors → Add custom connector**.
                2. **Name:** `AllowMe` · **Remote MCP server URL:** your full **magic link** (`https://allowme.dev/mcp?setup=SETUP-XXXX-XXXX`).
                3. **Add** → **Connect** → sign in.
              </Tab>
              <Tab title="ChatGPT">
                [Open ChatGPT connector settings](https://chatgpt.com/#settings/Connectors) — shortcut only; complete the steps below.

                1. **Settings → Apps → Advanced settings** → enable **Developer Mode** if prompted.
                2. **Create** → **New App**.
                3. **Name:** `AllowMe` · **Description:** `Family allowance and savings on Base` · **MCP Server URL:** your **magic link** · **Authentication:** `OAuth`
                4. Check **"I understand and want to continue"** → **Create** → sign in.

                <Note>
                  AllowMe is a developer-mode app today — it isn't listed in the
                  directory yet.
                </Note>
              </Tab>
              <Tab title="Claude Code">
                ```bash
                claude mcp add --transport http allowme "https://allowme.dev/mcp?setup=SETUP-XXXX-XXXX"
                ```
              </Tab>
              <Tab title="Codex">
                ```json
                {
                  "mcpServers": {
                    "allowme": { "url": "https://allowme.dev/mcp?setup=SETUP-XXXX-XXXX" }
                  }
                }
                ```
              </Tab>
              <Tab title="Cursor">
                Add to `~/.cursor/mcp.json`:

                ```json
                {
                  "mcpServers": {
                    "allowme": { "url": "https://allowme.dev/mcp?setup=SETUP-XXXX-XXXX" }
                  }
                }
                ```
              </Tab>
            </Tabs>
          </Step>

          <Step title="Set up your family">
            In Claude or ChatGPT (with AllowMe connected), paste:

            ```text
            Set up the Isaac family. Aiden gets $5/week, 100% education.
            ```

            That runs **configure-policy** and creates your family's wallet and rules.

            <Note>
              **One-time funding:** You'll fund your family treasury once — AllowMe
              shows you the address when you're ready. You don't need to fund before
              your first verify.
            </Note>
          </Step>

          <Step title="Try it">
            ```text
            Aiden finished his reading lesson — 85 out of 100, education
            How's Aiden doing?
            Distribute what Aiden earned
            ```

            When you **distribute** (or settle) earnings, **AllowMe will show you
            exactly what will happen and ask you to approve before anything moves.**
          </Step>
        </Steps>
      </Tab>

      <Tab title="I was invited by my partner">
        You're a **Co-parent** — you join a family that already exists. You can
        verify achievements and check progress. You **don't** set up the family or
        distribute earnings.

        <Steps>
          <Step title="Open your invite">
            1. Open **[allowme.dev/verify](https://allowme.dev/verify)**.
            2. Either:
               - Enter the **invite code** your partner sent (e.g. `SOFI-LEARN-X7K2`)
                 under **Have an invite code?** → **Look up invite**, or
               - Open the **invite link** they forwarded you.
            3. On the preview screen, click **Sign in with Base & join** and approve
               in your wallet.
            4. After you join, copy **your magic link** from the success screen
               (`https://allowme.dev/mcp?setup=SETUP-XXXX-XXXX`).
          </Step>

          <Step title="Connect AllowMe">
            <Tabs>
              <Tab title="Claude">
                **Fastest path:** [Open AllowMe in Claude](https://claude.ai/customize/connectors?modal=add-custom-connector&connectorName=AllowMe&connectorUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fallowme.dev%2Fmcp%3Fsetup%3DSETUP-XXXX-XXXX) — pre-fills your **magic link**.

                **No button?** Paste your full magic link into **Remote MCP server URL** → **Add** → **Connect** → sign in.
              </Tab>
              <Tab title="ChatGPT">
                [Open ChatGPT connector settings](https://chatgpt.com/#settings/Connectors) — shortcut only; then:

                1. **Settings → Apps → Advanced settings** → **Developer Mode**.
                2. **Create** → **New App**.
                3. **Name:** `AllowMe` · **MCP Server URL:** your **magic link** · **Authentication:** `OAuth`
                4. Risk checkbox → **Create** → sign in.

                <Note>Developer-mode app — not in the directory yet.</Note>
              </Tab>
            </Tabs>
          </Step>

          <Step title="Try it">
            ```text
            How is Aiden doing this week?
            Verify that Aiden read for 30 minutes today
            ```
          </Step>
        </Steps>
      </Tab>

      <Tab title="I was invited by my parent">
        You're a **Learner**. Open the link your parent sent — that's it. No wallet,
        no invite code to type if they sent a link, no family setup.

        <Steps>
          <Step title="Open the link your parent sent you">
            1. Tap the **invite link** from your parent (text, email, or QR).
            2. On **[allowme.dev/verify](https://allowme.dev/verify)**, confirm your
               name and tap **Join the family** — you won't need a wallet.
            3. On the success screen, tap **Open in Claude** (or copy your **magic
               link** for another app).

            Your link looks like
            `https://allowme.dev/mcp?setup=SETUP-XXXX-XXXX` — use the whole link,
            not `allowme.dev/mcp` by itself.

            <Note>
              No invite yet? Ask your parent. You're joining their family — you don't
              create one.
            </Note>
          </Step>

          <Step title="Try it">
            Ask AllowMe anything like:

            ```text
            How am I doing this week?
            How much have I earned?
            Move my earnings to my wallet
            How much is in my savings?
            ```

            When you **move your earnings**, **AllowMe will show you exactly what
            will happen and ask you to approve before anything moves.**
          </Step>
        </Steps>
      </Tab>

      <Tab title="I help a family with their finances">
        You're an **Advisor** — read-and-advise access to a family you support.

        <Steps>
          <Step title="Open your invite">
            1. Open **[allowme.dev/verify](https://allowme.dev/verify)**.
            2. Enter your **invite code** or open the link you were sent → **Look up
               invite**.
            3. Click **Sign in with Base & join** and approve in your wallet.
            4. Copy **your magic link** from the success screen.
          </Step>

          <Step title="Connect AllowMe">
            <Tabs>
              <Tab title="Claude">
                **Fastest path:** [Open AllowMe in Claude](https://claude.ai/customize/connectors?modal=add-custom-connector&connectorName=AllowMe&connectorUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fallowme.dev%2Fmcp%3Fsetup%3DSETUP-XXXX-XXXX)

                **No button?** Paste your magic link → **Add** → **Connect** → sign in.
              </Tab>
              <Tab title="ChatGPT">
                [Open ChatGPT connector settings](https://chatgpt.com/#settings/Connectors) — then:

                1. **Settings → Apps → Advanced settings** → **Developer Mode**.
                2. **Create** → **New App** · **Name:** `AllowMe` · **MCP Server URL:** your **magic link** · **Authentication:** `OAuth`
                3. Risk checkbox → **Create** → sign in.
              </Tab>
              <Tab title="Claude Code">
                ```bash
                claude mcp add --transport http allowme "https://allowme.dev/mcp?setup=SETUP-XXXX-XXXX"
                ```
              </Tab>
              <Tab title="Codex">
                ```json
                { "mcpServers": { "allowme": { "url": "https://allowme.dev/mcp?setup=SETUP-XXXX-XXXX" } } }
                ```
              </Tab>
              <Tab title="Cursor">
                ```json
                { "mcpServers": { "allowme": { "url": "https://allowme.dev/mcp?setup=SETUP-XXXX-XXXX" } } }
                ```
              </Tab>
            </Tabs>
          </Step>

          <Step title="Try it">
            ```text
            Show me this family's recent activity
            How much has each child earned this month?
            ```
          </Step>
        </Steps>
      </Tab>

      <Tab title="I'm family and want to follow along">
        You're a **Family viewer** — read-only. You can see how kids are doing; you
        can't change rules or move money.

        <Steps>
          <Step title="Open your invite">
            1. Open **[allowme.dev/verify](https://allowme.dev/verify)**.
            2. Enter your **invite code** or open the link you were sent → **Look up
               invite**.
            3. Click **Sign in with Base & join** and approve in your wallet.
            4. Copy **your magic link** from the success screen.
          </Step>

          <Step title="Connect AllowMe">
            <Tabs>
              <Tab title="Claude">
                **Fastest path:** [Open AllowMe in Claude](https://claude.ai/customize/connectors?modal=add-custom-connector&connectorName=AllowMe&connectorUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fallowme.dev%2Fmcp%3Fsetup%3DSETUP-XXXX-XXXX)

                **No button?** Paste your magic link → **Add** → **Connect** → sign in.
              </Tab>
              <Tab title="ChatGPT">
                [Open ChatGPT connector settings](https://chatgpt.com/#settings/Connectors) — shortcut only; then:

                1. **Settings → Apps → Advanced settings** → **Developer Mode**.
                2. **Create** → **New App**.
                3. **Name:** `AllowMe` · **MCP Server URL:** your **magic link** · **Authentication:** `OAuth`
                4. Risk checkbox → **Create** → sign in.

                <Note>Developer-mode app — not in the directory yet.</Note>
              </Tab>
            </Tabs>
          </Step>

          <Step title="Try it">
            ```text
            How is the family doing this week?
            Show me the family's recent achievements
            ```
          </Step>
        </Steps>
      </Tab>
    </Tabs>
  </Step>
</Steps>
