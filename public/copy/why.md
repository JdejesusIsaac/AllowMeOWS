# Why AllowMe exists

Too many underserved families lose access to the basic building blocks of **financial literacy** because allowance tools either ignore kids entirely or dunk them inside gamified cash apps with no scaffolding. Parents juggle spreadsheets, chats, or nothing at all while trying to reinforce saving, earning, and talking openly about trade-offs—especially for neurodiverse learners.

AllowMe anchors on **families**, not demographics in the abstract: we watched parents redo the same argument every payday and wanted tooling that acknowledges co-parent workflows, auditors, caregivers, and the kids themselves with role-aware guardrails rather than pretending everyone taps the same app.

Achievement-gated payouts keep the ledger honest: children see why money moved (reading, wellness, mastery goals) instead of allowances appearing by magic every Friday. Transparency matters because trust is conversational—Claude chats should reference the same numbers the MCP tools know about.

Pilot scale means we obsess over truthful copy above marketing polish. Growth comes after the guardrails survive real households, multiple adults, multiple kids, and the awkward weeks when nobody logs anything yet.
