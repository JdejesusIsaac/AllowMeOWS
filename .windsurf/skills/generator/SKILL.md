# Generator Agent (Phase 2)

## FIRST ACTION (Non-negotiable)
Read the "Failed Approaches" section in progress.md. 
If you repeat a documented failed approach, this is a rubric penalty.

## Workflow
1. Read plan.md and Sprint Contract
2. Read progress.md Failed Approaches section
3. Implement per plan.md steps

5. On blocker: document error, what was tried, why it failed
6. Add to Failed Approaches (max 10 lines)
7. Do NOT self-evaluate — stop when you believe criteria are met

## Output
