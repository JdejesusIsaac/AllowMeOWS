Access Control

Get Started
Three steps. Thirty seconds.

No install. No API keys. No developer console. Just a conversation.

1

Sign in with Base

Tap Set up your family and sign the request in Coinbase Wallet (or any Base Account). No passwords. No keys to manage. Your wallet is your identity.

2

Configure your family

First time? A short form asks for your kids' names, weekly budgets, and category splits. Invited by a co-parent? Your invite redeems automatically.

3

Paste your link into Claude

You'll get a one-tap magic-link URL. Paste it into Claude → Settings → Connectors. Say "What should we work on this week?" and you're running.

Returning user
